
🕵️ Cyber Detective: Digital Shadows

An Interactive Cybercrime Investigation & Mystery Game

Investigate digital crimes, solve logic puzzles, analyze evidence, interview suspects, and uncover the truth. Every decision matters, and your investigation determines how the story ends.

📌 About the Project

Cyber Detective: Digital Shadows is a futuristic, browser-based detective mystery game set in the year 2045.

A technology company called NovaTech reports that its confidential AI research has been stolen. As a digital detective, your mission is to investigate the incident, analyze digital evidence, question suspects, and identify the person responsible.

But the truth may be more complicated than it appears. Hidden clues could reveal a second crime that the company wants to keep secret.

The game combines mystery storytelling, logical reasoning, interactive puzzles, and decision-based gameplay.

🎮 Game Features

- Interactive Evidence Board: Collect clues and connect evidence to build your investigation.
- Branching Dialogue: Interview suspects and discover information based on your choices.
- Cybersecurity Puzzles: Solve fictional password challenges, decode messages, and analyze server logs.
- Four Different Endings: Your investigation and final decisions determine the outcome.
- Limited Hint System: Use hints strategically when you get stuck.
- Investigation Scoring: Earn points for evidence collection, correct deductions, and puzzle performance.
- Save and Continue: Save your progress and resume your investigation later.
- Achievements: Unlock rewards for completing special investigation challenges.
- Responsive Design: Play on desktop, tablet, or mobile browsers.

🏆 Multiple Endings

Ending| Description
Perfect Ending| Collect critical evidence and correctly identify the real culprit.
Partial Ending| Reach a plausible conclusion but leave important questions unanswered.
False Accusation| Accuse the wrong person or make an unsupported accusation.
Secret Ending| Discover hidden evidence revealing NovaTech's second crime.

Can you uncover the complete truth?

🚀 Getting Started

Prerequisites

You need a modern web browser, such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox.

No database server or complicated installation is required for the basic browser version.

Installation

1. Clone the repository

git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/cyber-detective-digital-shadows.git

2. Open the project folder

cd cyber-detective-digital-shadows

3. Run the game

If the project uses a standalone HTML entry point, open "index.html" in your browser.

Alternatively, use the VS Code Live Server extension for convenient local development.

🧩 How to Play

1. Start a new investigation.
2. Read the case briefing and learn about the suspects.
3. Explore digital files, messages, and server records.
4. Collect clues and solve the available puzzles.
5. Interview suspects and compare their statements with your evidence.
6. Connect relevant clues on the investigation board.
7. Build your theory and submit your final accusation.
8. Discover your ending and review your investigation score.

Tip: Do not rush to accuse the first suspicious person. Look for contradictions and collect evidence that supports your theory.

💾 Save System

The game uses browser LocalStorage to preserve supported gameplay progress.

Saved data may include:

- Collected evidence
- Completed puzzles
- Investigation stage
- Hint usage
- Score and achievements
- Ending history

Save data is stored locally in the browser and is not automatically synchronized between devices.

🔐 Safety and Privacy

All hacking challenges are fictional and designed for gameplay. The game is not intended to access real accounts, networks, or computer systems.

The basic version does not require users to enter real passwords or confidential information. LocalStorage data remains in the browser unless additional online functionality is implemented.
